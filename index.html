Notetaker

Strategic context

Discovery workshop for the Salesforce team to understand how Graff dealership works with General Motors and GM Financial.

Cross-cutting questions Ask everyone, ~2 min each

    1. General Manager / Dealer Principal 20 min

    What we're trying to learn: Strategic view of how the store runs end-to-end. Where the P&L pressure is, where growth is coming from, and what an ideal vendor/partner relationship looks like.

      2. Sales Manager / GSM 15 min

      What we're trying to learn: Lead-to-delivery flow, desking tools, sales-to-F&I handoff, and how customer/vehicle data moves between people on the showroom floor.

        3. F&I Manager 20 min

        What we're trying to learn: The full F&I session — menu tools, system stacking, what slows them down, and where deals get unwound or re-keyed.

          4. Service Manager 20 min

          What we're trying to learn: The service-lane-to-paid flow. Which systems they touch, where they sit on hold, and what's missing from a "single screen" view.

            5. Service Advisor 15 min

            What we're trying to learn: The front-line view. The advisor is the customer's voice. Probe customer lookup, communication, and where they get stuck.

              6. Parts Manager 15 min

              What we're trying to learn: Parts availability is a CSAT killer. How parts flow against open work orders, approvals, and recalls.

                7. BDC / Customer Experience Manager 15 min

                What we're trying to learn: Customer-facing comms, retention, and loyalty across sales, service, and finance touchpoints.

                  8. Office Manager / Controller 15 min

                  What we're trying to learn: The money side. How payments from outside parties hit the books, where reconciliation breaks, and what shouldn't have to be manual at all.
